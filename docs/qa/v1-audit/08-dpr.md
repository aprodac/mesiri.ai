# 08 · DPR — V1 audit

9 problems found in the DPR; the biggest is that any project added after signup never
sends a DPR: its automatic report is off and its clock is set to UTC, so no evening check
and no 9 PM PDF happen.

- **Audited:** 24 Oct 2026, `main` at `20bf7740` · **Original sheet:** [DPR — WhatsApp test sheet](https://claude.ai/artifact/5B7zxsoAiLfmrBiN8us7VA)
- **Not verified:** the live AI, real WhatsApp delivery, and the PDF as Chromium draws it on the server.
- **Not repeated here** (logged in other modules): absent workers counted (ATT-01), later-day progress missing (WRK-01), equipment / weather / plan empty (ISS-01..03), no photos (PHO-02).

## Before you test

A full day, up to 9 PM, and three phones.

- Phone 1: the Owner (Admin) who signed up. Project A from signup has one site.
- Phone 2: a Site Engineer on Project A's site and on both sites of Project B.
- Phone 3: a Project Manager of Project B.
- Create Project B on the dashboard (New project) with Site 1 and Site 2. Don't open its settings.
- Note each project's DPR time, send time and time zone (Projects & Sites → project → Reporting).
- For DPR-02: an Owner or client contact who hasn't messaged Mesiri for 24+ hours, added as a DPR recipient.

During the day the engineer sends work, labour and materials for every site. Check the
evening message to the engineer, the 9 PM PDF on the Owner's and PM's phones, and
Dashboard → Daily Reports.

## Problems

### DPR-01 · A project added after signup sends no DPR

- **Severity:** Serious · **Approval:** None for new defaults; **Alan: data rewrite** to fix existing projects' `reporting_timezone` / `auto_generate_dpr`
- **Repro:** create Project B; send work and labour for Site 1; wait until after 9 PM.
- **Actual:** the Reporting tab shows "UTC" and the automatic DPR off. No evening message, no PDF. If it is turned on but left on UTC, the cutoff runs at 11:30 PM and the DPR arrives at 2:30 AM.
- **Code:**
    - `ProjectCreate` in `backend/src/mesiri/domains/projects/router.py`: `reportingTimezone: str = "UTC"`, `autoGenerateDpr: bool = False`; the dashboard's `createProject` (`apps/dashboard/src/lib/projects.ts`) sends neither.
    - WhatsApp `create_project`: the INSERT in `backend/src/mesiri/infrastructure/postgres/repositories/project_execution.py` sets neither, so migration 0260's defaults apply (`'UTC'`, `false`).
    - Only signup's first project gets `Asia/Kolkata` + auto on (`backend/src/mesiri/application/organizations/create_company.py`, `signup_journey.py` `auto_dpr=True`).
    - `due_projects_for_auto_dpr` / `due_project_sends` (`auto_dpr.py`) fall back to `"UTC"`.
- **Evidence (scripted run):** `ProjectCreate(name=…)` → `timezone: UTC | auto DPR: False`; `is_due(…"18:00", "UTC")` first true at 24 Oct 23:30 IST; `send_reason(…"21:00")` first true at 25 Oct 02:30 IST.
- **Fix direction:** new projects take the company's timezone and auto DPR on; validate the timezone field (DSH-03); one-off backfill for existing projects (needs Alan).

### DPR-02 · No DPR for anyone quiet for 24 hours

- **Severity:** Serious · **Approval:** Ops (tracker WA-10)
- **Repro:** use the recipient who has been quiet for 24 hours.
- **Actual:** no PDF at 9 PM; the notification is skipped with `template_not_approved:mesiri_dpr_delivery`. Clients rarely message, so they would never get it.
- **Code:** `choose_template` in `apps/whatsapp-assistant/src/channel/whatsapp/templates.py` (needs `WHATSAPP_APPROVED_TEMPLATES`).

### DPR-03 · Multi-site project DPR lists no work; labour table empty

- **Severity:** Serious · **Approval:** Decision (what the owner's report should hold)
- **Repro:** Project B, Site 1: `2nd floor plastering 70%`, `3rd floor slab concreting done`, `5 mason, 8 helper`. Confirm Site 1.
- **Actual:** the 9 PM PDF has no "plastering" or "slab concreting" anywhere. The Labour table reads "No attendance recorded for this date" next to "Workers Today 13". Tomorrow's plan and weather are left out.
- **Code:** `build_project_report_payload` in `backend/src/mesiri/application/dpr/assembly.py` keeps only per-site counts (no `activities`, `labour.trades: []`, no `conditions`); `template.py` hides the Activities table when `is_project`.
- **Evidence (scripted run):** project payload keys have no `activities`; `build_document_data` → `activities rows: 0 | trades: [] | headcount: 13 | plan: ''`; rendered HTML has no "plastering", and "Labour (Total cost: 0) Trade Headcount No attendance recorded for this date".
- **Fix direction:** a section per reported site with its work, trades and plan (or attach each site PDF).

### DPR-04 · An unconfirmed site is sent as "Not reported"

- **Severity:** Serious · **Approval:** Decision (changes the agreed P8 rule: the roll-up uses confirmed sites only)
- **Repro:** Project A: send work and labour, don't tap Confirm all, wait for 9 PM.
- **Actual:** "Today's DPR (…) is attached. Site A: 2 updates, not confirmed yet." The PDF shows Site A "Not reported", Activities 0, Workers 0.
- **Code:** `finalize_project_dpr` (`auto_dpr.py`) sends the site DPR only when it is frozen; otherwise the roll-up passes `None` for the site to `build_project_report_payload`.
- **Fix direction:** at the deadline, include unconfirmed sites' drafts, clearly marked "Not confirmed by the engineer".

### DPR-05 · Owner/PM told "Nothing's been logged" during the day

- **Severity:** Serious · **Approval:** None
- **Repro:** after the engineer's entries, from the Owner at 4 PM: `today's DPR?`
- **Actual:** "Nothing's been logged for today yet, so there's no report. Send me today's update and I'll make it." (with 2 projects, or 1 project with 2 sites).
- **Code:** `_dpr_scope` (`seeding.py`) resolves no project for 2+ projects and no site for 2+ sites; `find_report` (`apps/whatsapp-assistant/src/runtime/dpr_request_query.py`) then returns `not_generated`; `_past_day_draft` (`query_entry.py`) needs a site; `"not_generated"` text in `workflows/dpr_request/nodes.py`.
- **Evidence (scripted run):** `_seed_dpr_request` + `generate_dpr_request_reply` for both actors → the reply above.
- **Fix direction:** for an owner/PM, answer with each site's "so far today" lines (or ask which project/site with buttons); never "nothing logged" when sites have entries.

### DPR-06 · "Total cost: 0" on every DPR

- **Severity:** Other · **Approval:** None (template only; wages themselves are Labour)
- **Repro:** `5 mason, 8 helper` → Save, then `today's DPR?`
- **Actual:** the Labour heading reads "Labour (Total cost: 0)". Wages are not in V1.
- **Code:** `template.py`: `Labour{% if data.headcount %} (Total cost: {{ data.labour_cost }}){% endif %}`.

### DPR-07 · "Confirm all" shows only counts

- **Severity:** Other · **Approval:** None
- **Repro:** a normal day; wait for the evening message.
- **Actual:** "• Work: 2 items", "• Labour: 13 workers", no work names, with a Confirm all button.
- **Code:** `day_lines` / `confirm_message` in `apps/whatsapp-assistant/src/runtime/site_day_review.py`.

### DPR-08 · A labour-only unconfirmed site is called "not reported"

- **Severity:** Other · **Approval:** None
- **Repro:** Project B, Site 2: only `4 helper` and `20 bags cement came`. Don't confirm.
- **Actual:** the 9 PM message says "1 of 2 sites not reported" for Site 2.
- **Code:** `_unconfirmed_updates` / `_coverage_line` in `project_auto_dpr.py` count `payload ->> 'activity_count'` only.

### DPR-09 · Dashboard "Download PDF" is a plain, different report

- **Severity:** Other · **Approval:** None
- **Repro:** Dashboard → Daily Reports → today, before 9 PM → Download PDF.
- **Actual:** no logo or letterhead (unlike the WhatsApp PDF), codes like "EQUIPMENT_BREAKDOWN", fails on Malayalam (ISS-07, ISS-13).
- **Code:** `get_daily_report_pdf` (`backend/src/mesiri/domains/dpr/router.py`) falls back to `render_dpr_pdf_bytes` (`backend/src/mesiri/domains/dpr/pdf.py`, fpdf) when no rendered PDF is stored.

## Already works

The engineer's "today's DPR?" during the day gets a draft PDF marked "Draft · not confirmed
yet"; "yesterday's DPR" and a date work; a day it cannot read ("last Friday") is asked about,
not guessed; the evening message picks missing / ready / no-update correctly; "Site closed
today" marks no work; with no engineer on a site, the evening message goes to whoever saved
that day; Confirm all is held back for possible repeats and forwarded messages; entries
after a day was confirmed appear under "Late entries for earlier days"; a confirmed
single-site day sends the full site DPR; typed symbols like "<" don't break the PDF
(Jinja `autoescape=True`).
