The automated tests cover each of these with stand-ins. They have not been seen on real WhatsApp. Use a test phone number that has never signed up, and a second number for a team member.

  1. A new owner is asked, once

    DoSign up a new number on WhatsApp (Set up my company), then send a site update.

    SeeAfter Mesiri's reply, the question above with a Choose list of four rows. Titles fit without “…”.

  2. Each answer saves and gives its tip

    DoTap an answer. On the dashboard, open the ? at the top.

    SeeThe reply shown in the phone here; the dashboard's “How Mesiri guides you” shows the same answer and asks nothing.

  3. Answered on the dashboard first guard

    DoNew web signup, answer on the welcome screen, then message Mesiri.

    SeeNo start question on WhatsApp; the next setup question (letterhead) as before.

  4. Not in the middle of something guard

    DoAs a new owner, send 20 bags cement came and do not tap Save.

    SeeNo question while the card waits; it comes after a later reply.

  5. An existing owner is never asked guard

    DoMessage from an owner account older than two weeks.

    SeeNothing new on WhatsApp; no dialog on the dashboard.

  6. A team member is not asked on WhatsApp

    DoFrom a site engineer's number, send an update.

    SeeNo question on WhatsApp (setup questions are for the owner). The dialog appears on their first dashboard visit.

  7. Changing it in chat

    DoSend no more tips, then tips venda, then explain step by step.

    SeeSet at once, no Save card, a short confirmation; the dashboard's ? panel follows.

  8. A record in the same message still waits guard

    DoSend no more tips, and 10 bags cement came.

    SeeThe tip setting changes now; the cement waits on the card for Save.

  9. The day after

    DoNext day, message again as the same owner.

    SeeThe letterhead question, then report time, then more sites, one a day, as before.

  10. Control panel

    DoOpen Control panel → Trials.

    SeeThe test trials show their answer under the company name, and the “By how the owner chose to start” table counts them.

Known gaps to watch for

  • The question and the four tips are in English, like the other setup questions today. A Manglish or Malayalam owner gets them in English.
  • Only the first tip is sent. The week of daily tips is not built yet (SS-38).
  • The confirmation after no more tips is written by the model, so its words vary.
