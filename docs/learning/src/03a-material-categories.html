1 · What a material category is

A shelf in the store, for hundreds of items

A mid-size building project uses a few hundred different items: cement and steel, but also 2-way switches, 20 mm elbows, 6A sockets, CP taps, tile spacers. A category is the shelf an item sits on, so people can find it, count it, total it and give it to the right person. It does not change what the item is; it groups it.

Four words that are easy to mix up

WordWhat it isExample
Main categoryThe trade or big group.Electrical
Sub-categoryA group inside it.Electrical › Switches & sockets
ItemOne thing you buy, count and use. Each size or type is its own item.2-way switch 6A · 1-way switch 6A
UnitWhat one item is counted in.nos, bags, cft, kg, rolls
The rule that keeps it simple: sizes and types are items, not categories. "2-way switch 6A" and "1-way switch 6A" are two items under one sub-category. If sizes became categories, the tree would grow to thousands of shelves with one item each.

3 · How construction companies sort material

By trade is the most common; companies change the names

SchemeLooks likeWhen it is used
By trade (Mesiri's default)Structural · Electrical · Plumbing & CP · Fire · Gas · Lift · FinishingMost builders and contractors; matches who installs it and who sells it.
By work sectionEarthwork · Concrete · Masonry · Plastering · Flooring · PaintingCompanies that think in BOQ sections; good for cost comparison.
By stageFoundation · Superstructure · Finishing · External worksDevelopers planning cash by stage.
By accounting groupRaw material · Consumables · Tools · SparesAccounts and the CA; often in Tally.

Trade is the best starting point because it matches three things at once: who supplies it, who installs it, and how people talk about it on site. A company can then rename, add and switch off categories to fit its own habits. The other schemes are usually needed alongside it (work section for cost, accounting group for the CA), which is why part 8 links categories to cost centres instead of replacing them.

Good rules for a category list

  • Two levels are enough for a mid-size builder: main and sub. A third level is where lists become unusable.
  • One item, one place. An item sits in exactly one category; reports never double count.
  • The company owns the list. Rename "Plumbing & CP" to "Plumbing" if that is what they say.
  • Never delete a used category. Switch it off: old items keep it, new items can't pick it.
  • "No category" is visible, never hidden, so someone places those items.
  • Never guess. A wrong category is worse than none: "Stainless steel sink" is not Steel, "Sand paper" is not Masonry.

4 · Mesiri's model: tables and rules

One table for the tree, one column on each item

RuleHow it is enforced
Per companyEvery row has organization_id; row-level security (as every company table) keeps one company from seeing another's.
Two levelsA row with no parent is a main category; a sub's parent must be a main one. The API refuses a sub under a sub ("already a sub-category; add this under its main category").
Unique names per parentA unique index on (company, parent, lower-case name): "Pipes" can exist under Plumbing and under Fire protection, but not twice under Plumbing.
Switch off, never loseis_active = false: items keep it, it is no longer offered. Delete works only for an empty category; otherwise 409 "move them out first, or switch it off".
AdditiveMigration 0503 added the table and a nullable category_id; the old free-text category column was kept, not rewritten, and is now only a hint.
Main includes subsFiltering by a main category (stock, deliveries, usage) includes every sub under it.

5 · How a material gets its category

Four ways in, and one rule above them: never guess

WayWhat happensExample
1 Picked on the dashboardItems tab: a category picker on each item; a "No category" list with a picker and the old free-text group shown as a hint.Wash basin → Plumbing & CP › Sanitaryware
2 Excel importColumns Main category, Sub-category, Item, Unit, Item code, Other names. New sub-categories are added; existing items without a category get the one in the file.A 400-row item list loaded at setup
3 Standard list for existing itemsWhen a company adds the standard list, items are placed only by a starter name or an exact old group; anything else stays under No category."Masonry" (old group) → Structural › Masonry
4 Suggested for a new itemWhen someone adds a new material (dashboard or WhatsApp), Mesiri suggests its category; it shows on the card before Save."MCB 32A" → Electrical › DB & MCBs

How the suggestion works (and why it is strict)

NameResultWhy
CementStructural › MasonryA starter item's name (the starter list files cement under Masonry)
Binding wire 18 gaugeStructural › SteelA starter item; sizes ("18 gauge") are ignored
MCB 32AElectrical › DB & MCBsEvery meaningful word belongs to that one category
Stainless steel sinkNot Steel. Sanitaryware, from the AI's hint"Stainless" is unknown to the rule, so the rule places nothing; the AI read the whole name
Sand paperNot Masonry"Paper" is unknown: the rule places nothing
GI pipe 1 inchOnly by the AI's hint, or none"Pipe" is in Plumbing, Fire and Gas: a shared word places nothing

The order matters: the AI's hint first (it reads the whole name in context), then the strict rule, then nothing. The AI may only name one of the company's own switched-on categories; it can never create one. This was tightened on 5 Nov after real mistakes ("Stainless steel sink" went to Steel).

The API (what the dashboard and the WhatsApp assistant both call)

RouteWhat it does
GET /materials/categoriesThe company's tree in order, items per category, and the count with no category.
POST /materials/categoriesAdd a main category, or a sub with parent_id.
PATCH /materials/categories/{id}Rename, reorder (sort_order), switch off (is_active).
DELETE /materials/categories/{id}Delete only if empty; else 409.
POST /materials/categories/standardAdd the standard builder list and place items by fixed rules; safe to repeat.
GET /materials/categories/suggest?name&hintThe suggestion of part 5, read-only.
POST/PATCH /materials with category_idPut an item in a category, or take it out (null).
GET /materials/inventory|stock-summary|inflows|outflows?category_id=Stock, period stock, deliveries, use filtered by category (main includes subs); rows carry category and main_category.

The assistant uses the same routes as tools (material_categories, add_standard_categories, stock_levels with category_id, update_material with a category in words). One rule in one place: the API decides; the dashboard and WhatsApp only show it.

9 · Terms

Main category
The top level: a trade or big group (Electrical).
Sub-category
A group under a main category (Switches & sockets).
Item
One thing bought, counted and used; each size or type its own item.
Standard list
The 7 main and 27 sub-categories every new company starts with.
Switched off
A category no longer offered; items keep it.
No category
Items not yet placed; shown so someone places them.
Hint
The AI's category in words, checked against the company's own list.
HSN code
The tax classification code used on GST bills.
Cost centre
The bucket a cost is reported in (materials, labour, fuel); not the same as a material category.
