## What can go wrong

### 15. Five ways a job starts badly

*What can go wrong · leaks*

On screen:

- No WhatsApp number · Day 1's messages go nowhere
- Drawings unchecked · Roof +660 or +655, at the pour
- No least-stock · Cement out on a Friday
- Old balance not in · Colour World paid twice
- No dates · Late against what?
- Each one costs minutes now, and weeks later.

Narration:

> Five ways a job starts badly.
>
> A number missing: the site's messages go nowhere, and the office finds out a week later.
>
> Drawings not checked: the roof level is argued about on the day of the pour.
>
> No least-stock: the cement runs out on a Friday, and the masons go home.
>
> An old balance not put in: a supplier is paid twice, or argues for a year. And no dates: nobody can say whether the job is late.
