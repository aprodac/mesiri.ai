## On a real site

### 2. Five records, and what each one protects

*On a real site · story*

On screen:

- Attendance · Wages: pay for who came, and only them
- Work done · The bill: counted on its BOQ line
- Material used · Stock: came, minus used, should be there
- Small spends · Petty cash: tea, diesel, nails
- Conditions · Why a day was slow: rain, hours lost, breakdowns
- Written the same day, they are facts. Remembered later, they are guesses.

Narration:

> A site day leaves five kinds of record. Each one protects something.
>
> Attendance protects the wages: pay for the people who came, and only them.
>
> Work done protects the bill. It is counted on its BOQ line, and at the month's end, the client pays for exactly that.
>
> Material used protects the stock. What came, minus what was used, is what should still be there.
>
> The day's small spends protect the petty cash: tea, diesel, nails. Small, every day, and gone by the month's end if nobody writes them.
>
> And the day's conditions: rain, hours lost, a machine down. They explain a slow day, today and when someone asks why the job was late.

Narration:

> A crew counted as six when four came. Two days' wages for nobody, every day, all year.
>
> Material used but not said: the stock on paper is more than the stock on site, and nobody knows where it went.
