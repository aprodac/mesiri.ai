# Images for the sample project: prompts for ChatGPT

> Asked 20 Nov 2026: "we are simulating a project ... we also need images of the project when it is in different
> stages, when materials come, or any work a role is doing. I will create them from ChatGPT: give me the prompts."
> Every prompt follows the project bible (`PROJECT_BIBLE.md`), the drawing set (`green_villa.py`) and the 62-day plan
> (`docs/plans/sample-plan.md`), so a picture never shows something the story, the drawings or the figures contradict.

## How to use these

1. **Start a new ChatGPT chat for the series** and paste the **style block** first, once. Then paste one prompt at a time.
2. **Make the two reference pictures first** (R1, R2), then for every Green Villa picture say: *"Same house as the
   reference picture."* Attach the reference when ChatGPT allows it. This keeps the building the same in every image.
3. **Phone photos** (what Ravi sends on WhatsApp): ask for **4:3, portrait or landscape**. **Film stills** (episode
   openings): ask for **16:9 landscape**.
4. **Save each image under its file name** (the first column) in a folder `site-photos/`, and send them to me. I'll
   put them in the starter kit and the simulation's day photos, so each one appears on the day it belongs to.
5. **Check every picture against its "must show" line** before keeping it. Redo it if the storeys, the columns or the
   materials are wrong: a builder watching will notice.
6. **No real brands:** no cement, steel or paint logos, no company names on trucks or bags. Plain bags, plain trucks.

## The style block (paste once at the start)

```
You are making realistic photographs for a training course about running a building site in Kerala, India.
Style: real smartphone photos taken by a site engineer on site, natural daylight, slightly imperfect framing,
no filters, no text overlays, no watermarks, no brand logos anywhere (plain unbranded cement bags, plain trucks).
Setting: Thrissur, Kerala: red laterite soil, coconut palms, green vegetation, humid air, low compound walls,
neighbouring two-storey Kerala houses in the background.
People: Malayali site workers and engineers, and a few migrant helpers from Bengal; work clothes (shirts, lungis
or trousers, rubber slippers or boots), some in yellow helmets; faces natural and not posed, often seen side-on or
from behind while working. Nobody looks at the camera unless the prompt says so.
Construction: RCC frame with columns and beams, solid concrete block walls, cement plaster, bamboo and steel props,
wooden and steel shuttering plates. Everything must look like a real, slightly untidy working site, not a render.
```

## The building, as drawn (tell ChatGPT these facts when it drifts)

- **Green Villa, Main block:** two storeys, flat RCC roof with a parapet, about 14 m wide and 8 m deep. At the front
  left, a **sit-out** with **two square columns** and **three steps**; above it a **balcony** with an MS railing. At the
  front right, the ground floor only, with an **open terrace** on its roof behind a low parapet. UPVC windows with
  concrete **sunshades**. Pale walls once painted.
- **Green Villa, Annex:** a small **single-storey** block beside the Main block, built from the plinth during the story.
- **Lake View Apartments:** two **residential towers** (Tower A, Tower B) in Kakkanad, Kochi: a basement, a ground
  floor and six upper floors each; RCC frame going up floor by floor; a tower crane is not needed (concrete comes by
  ready-mix truck and pump).
