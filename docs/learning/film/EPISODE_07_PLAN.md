## 1. Objective

From the series plan, unchanged:

> By the end you can pay a subcontractor only for measured work: work order → measurement → bill → retention and TDS
> → payment.

The viewer's test: *Joy Tiling sends a bill for 92 square metres. Say what you check it against, who measured what,
what is held back and why, and what is paid.*

## 2. What we found planning it

| | The sample company's 62 days (before) | The starter kit's papers |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | ₹95 a sqm (too low for laying vitrified tiles) | ₹320 a sqm, labour only |
| Measurement | 30 sqm a day, measured every Saturday | the finished ground floor, room by room from A-101 rev B: 81.88 sqm |
| Bill | always exactly what was measured | JTW-014 asks for 92 sqm: 10.12 sqm (₹3,238) more than measured |
| TDS | off in the sample company | not on the paper |

The lesson was on paper only, as in episodes 4 and 6. The story is played first.

TDS is switched on in the sample company's finance settings (1%, a proprietor's firm). Retention stays held until the
defects period ends (episode 11).

## 4. The episode (about 7 minutes)

Cold open (Joy's bill on Meera's desk: 92) · On a real site (what a subcontract is; measured, not claimed; retention;
TDS) · Who does what · In Mesiri: the work order (owner, day 33); the measurement (Anil, day 38); the bill flagged
(Meera, day 39); turned down (Anil, day 39); the corrected bill and its deductions (Meera, day 40) · What the owner
sees: the work order on day 49, what was stopped, paid and held · What can go wrong · Three questions.

## 5. Questions, with the answer built

1. **Who records the measurement?** Anil, on the work order; Ravi measures with Joy on site (a measurement is the
   office's record of the site's tape). The narration says so.
2. **TDS rate:** 1% (194C, an individual or proprietor); a company would be 2%. Said once.
3. **The kit's dates** (measured 28 Nov, bill 29 Nov) do not match the sample company's days; not changed (as in
   episode 6).
