The basics

Four words are enough

Footing: the wide concrete block buried under each pillar. Spreads the weight on the ground.
Column: the pillar standing on the footing.
Beam and slab: beams join the columns; the slab is the floor and the roof.
Concrete: m³
Steel: kg
Cement: bags
